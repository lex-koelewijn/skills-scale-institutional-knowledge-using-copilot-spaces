## Process overview
OctoAcme runs projects through a clear lifecycle that starts with initiation, where teams confirm the business need, define measurable success metrics, identify stakeholders, and decide whether the work should move forward. Planning then turns the approved initiative into a prioritized backlog, a release and milestone plan, a Definition of Done, and an initial view of risks, dependencies, and QA needs.

Execution focuses on small, testable increments managed through a shared project board and pull request workflow. Teams track progress against milestones and success metrics, review blockers in regular team syncs, and keep quality embedded in delivery through unit, integration, smoke, and manual QA practices as needed. Risks, dependencies, and status updates are maintained as ongoing artifacts rather than one-time planning outputs.

Release and deployment emphasize readiness and risk reduction: acceptance criteria must be met, CI and security checks should pass, release notes and rollback plans should be prepared, and staging plus post-deployment verifications should be completed. After each sprint, release, milestone, or incident, OctoAcme captures lessons learned in retrospectives, limits improvement work to a small number of owned action items, and tracks those items through the backlog or issue system.

## Roles at a glance
- **Project Manager**: coordinates schedules, delivery tracking, risks, dependencies, documentation, and stakeholder communication.
- **Product Manager**: defines the problem, owns prioritization, aligns work to customer value, and tracks success metrics.
- **Developers**: design, implement, test, review, and help surface technical risks and dependencies.
- **QA/Testing**: validates acceptance criteria and overall release quality through the appropriate mix of automated and manual testing.
- **Stakeholders**: provide business context, input, approvals, and ongoing feedback.
